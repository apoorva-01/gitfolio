What's the personality of GitFolio?

This determines everything — typography, color, layout rhythm, icon style. Pick the one that feels most like YOU.

A

Premium Minimal

Think Linear, Vercel, Raycast. Monochromatic with a single accent. Tons of whitespace. Every pixel earns its place.

B

Warm Editorial

Like a well-designed print magazine. Serif headlines, warm off-white tones, generous margins. Feels human and crafted.

C

Dev Brutalist

In-your-face like a terminal. Monospace fonts, high contrast, visible grid lines. Raw but intentional. For devs who hate "pretty".

D

Vibrant & Bold

Loud gradients, bold type, energetic motion. Like a well-designed fintech app. Stands out in a sea of dark-purple gradients.
