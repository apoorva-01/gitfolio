Who is the primary audience?

Affects tone, content, and feature emphasis throughout.

A

Tech Recruiters & Hiring Managers

They skim fast. Need clear proof of competence and culture fit. Visual polish matters to impress them.

B

Other Developers

They appreciate code quality, open source contributions, and interesting projects. Technical credibility is the currency.

C

Both — it's a portfolio that converts

Has to impress non-technical visitors AND prove technical chops to engineers. The hardest but most valuable target.
