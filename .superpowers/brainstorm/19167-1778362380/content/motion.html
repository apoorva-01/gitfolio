Motion & Animation?

How much life should GitFolio have? Affects feel and perceived polish.

A

Minimal — micro only

Subtle hover states, smooth page transitions. No decorative animation. Feels fast and professional.

B

Moderate — entrance choreography

Cards fade + slide in on load, numbers count up, staggered reveals. Adds polish without distraction.

C

Generous — ambient motion

Particles, parallax, animated gradients. Feels premium but risks feeling "built by AI".
