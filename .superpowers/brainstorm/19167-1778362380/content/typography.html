Which typeface for headlines?

The headline font sets the entire tone of Premium Minimal.

A

Geist

Tight tracking, geometric, modern. Vercel's typeface. Technical and precise.

B

Satoshi

Slightly softer, more approachable. Rounded terminals but still clean. Linear's feel.

C

Switzer

Slightly wider, warmer. More editorial character. Stands out from tech crowd.

D

General Sans

Balanced, refined, distinctive. More personality than Geist without losing minimalism.
